SonarQube Connector for Jira

Centralise your SonarQube quality and security insights in Jira. Monitor project health, investigate issues, create remediation work items, and share PDF reports without leaving your Jira workflow.

SonarQube Connector for Jira is an app developed by excentia that helps development teams bring SonarQube code quality and security data into their Jira projects.

Key capabilities

  • View security, reliability, maintainability, coverage, and technical debt metrics directly in Jira.
  • Explore SonarQube issues and create Jira work items to organise remediation.
  • Access security reports and download PDF reports for stakeholders.
  • Keep development teams aligned around code quality from a single Jira-based experience.
