We inform you that our organisation complies with European and Spanish data protection legislation (GDPR 2016/679 and Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights). Accordingly, your personal data will only be processed to maintain our relationship with you, respond to your requests, send you commercial communications and provide our services. Your data will be retained for as long as this relationship continues, as required by law, and for as long as necessary to address any liabilities arising from such processing. Your data will not be disclosed to third parties unless required by law or strictly necessary to provide our services or respond to your requests. You may contact us at any time to object to the processing of your data, find out what personal data we hold about you, restrict its use, rectify it or request its deletion. You also have the right to request the transfer of your information to another organisation. To exercise any of these rights, please write to: <strong>dpd@excentia.es</strong>.

If you believe that any of the above rights have not been respected or that your right to personal data protection has been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos), at C/ Jorge Juan, 6, 28001 Madrid, Spain, or via www.aepd.es. Further information is available in our <a href="privacy">Privacy Policy</a>.
